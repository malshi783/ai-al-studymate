# 📚 AI A/L StudyMate

AI A/L StudyMate is a Flutter-based mobile application designed to help Sri Lankan G.C.E. Advanced Level students organize their studies, manage their workload, and build better study habits.

The application is being developed with a focus on providing students with a simple and practical platform to manage school work, tuition classes, assignments, revision, past papers, and study plans.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main goal of AI A/L StudyMate is to help A/L students organize their daily academic activities in one place.

The application aims to reduce the difficulty of managing:

- 📖 School work
- 📝 Assignments
- 🏫 Tuition classes
- ⏰ Study schedules
- 📚 Revision
- 📄 Past papers
- 🎯 Exam preparation
- 🤖 Personalized study planning
